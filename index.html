WAFD MATES

Wholehearted Active Friends for Da’wah

WAFD MATES is the name of the 39th batch of Darul Huda Islamic University (DHIU).

The batch consists of 45 students guided by two respected teachers:

  1. Vajeeh Hudawi Usthad
  2. Shahin Ali Hudawi Usthad

WAFD MATES represents a group of wholehearted, active friends united by a shared commitment to Da’wah, knowledge, brotherhood, and Islamic values.
